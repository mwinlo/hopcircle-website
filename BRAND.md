# HopCircle — Brand Guidelines

---

## 1. Brand Essence

**Tagline:** Playdates without the awkward ask.

**What we are:** A playdate matching app that connects families at the same school. Parents post a Hop (my kid wants to play) or open a Circle (I can host), and HopCircle makes the match.

**What we're not:** A babysitting service, a social network, a dating app for parents, or a kids' app. We're a tool for parents who want their children to play more.

**Brand personality:** Warm, approachable, slightly cheeky, reassuringly simple. Like the confident mum at school pickup who just makes things happen.

### App (iOS)

| Style | Font | Size | Weight |
|-------|------|------|--------|
| Screen title | Nunito | 22pt | ExtraBold (800) |
| App name | Nunito | 28pt | ExtraBold (800) |
| Section header | Nunito | 18pt | Bold (700) |

| Body | Nunito | 15pt | Regular (400) |
| Caption | Nunito | 13pt | Medium (500) |
| Button | Nunito | 16pt | Bold (700) |
| Badges/chips | SF Pro | 9–10pt | Bold/SemiBold |

### Website

| Style | Font | Weight |
|-------|------|--------|
| Headings | Nunito | ExtraBold (800) |
| Body | Inter | Regular (400) |
| Buttons | Nunito | Bold (700) |

### Rules
- Nunito is our brand typeface. Its rounded terminals match our friendly, approachable tone.
- Inter is used for body text on the web where readability at small sizes matters.
- Never use sharp serifs, thin/light weights, or decorative fonts.
- Never set body text in all caps. Short labels and badges may be uppercase sparingly.

## 6. UI Components

### Cards
- **Background:** White (light) or Dark Card (dark mode)
- **Corner radius:** 16pt
- **Shadow:** `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`
- **Left accent border:** 4pt wide, rounded on the left corners
  - Teal for Hop cards
  - Coral for Circle cards
  - Sunshine for confirmed HopCircle cards

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Coral background, white text, 12pt radius, full width, spring-press animation (0.96 scale)
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, coral text, 1pt coral border at 30% opacity, 12pt radius
- **Accept ("We're in!"):** Teal background, white text, spring-press

### Backgrounds
- Always warm cream (`#FFF8F0`) or warm grey (`#F5F0EB`) — never stark white (`#FFFFFF`)
- Cards are white on cream backgrounds for contrast
- Gradient overlays use coral-to-teal at 135 degrees for hero moments

## 7. Photography & Imagery

- **Children:** Always shown from behind, as silhouettes, or illustrated. Never identifiable faces.
- **Aesthetic:** Warm, sun-drenched, Perth outdoor feel. Golden light, backyards, parks, neighbourhood streets.
- **Illustrations:** Watercolour textures and soft, hand-drawn feels are preferred over sharp vector graphics.
- **AI-generated images:** Must follow the brand colour palette. Always add a warm colour grade. See `SOCIAL-CAMPAIGN-PROMPT.md` for generation style prompts.

## 8. Voice & Tone

### We sound like...
A friend who gets it. Warm but not gushing. Practical but not boring. Slightly cheeky — like a knowing smile.

### We write in...
- Australian English: organise, neighbourhood, favourite, mum, colour
- Short sentences. Punchy. Line breaks for rhythm.
- Second person ("Your kid wants to play") or first person plural ("We built this because...")

### Vocabulary

| Use | Don't use |
|-----|-----------|
| Hop | Availability slot |
| Circle | Hosting session |
| HopCircle (noun) | Confirmed match |
| Families | Users |
| Kids, children | Minors |
| Playdate | Play date, play-date |
| Mates | Contacts, connections |
| "Hop in!" | "Join", "Accept" (in UI copy) |
| "We're in!" | "Confirm" (in UI copy) |
| "It's a HopCircle!" | "Match confirmed" |

### Key phrases
- "Playdates without the awkward ask"
- "Your neighbourhood, your circle"
- "Hop in"
- "Post a Hop. Open a Circle."

---

## 9. Motion & Animation

- **Spring press:** Buttons scale to 0.96 on press with spring response 0.3, damping 0.6
- **Staggered appear:** Cards and list items fade up (20pt) with 0.05s stagger per item
- **Float gentle:** Decorative elements float ±8px over 6 seconds
- **Pulsing:** Unread indicators pulse scale 1.0–1.3 with opacity 1.0–0.7
- **Celebrations:** The "It's a HopCircle!" moment uses bounce-in (scale 0.3→1.05→0.95→1.0)
